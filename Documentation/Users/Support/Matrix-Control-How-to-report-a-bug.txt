WHAT IS THIS DOCUMENT FOR?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This text is for day-to-day product use: a bug, odd behavior or a specific
question, outside a structured beta test session.

During a beta test campaign, prefer the filled test guide
(Matrix-Control-Beta-Test-Guide.txt): it already contains the session
sheet, checked boxes and notes. Simply attach that file by email (see the
"Send your results" section of the guide).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT INFORMATION TO INCLUDE IN YOUR MESSAGE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Include at least:

1. Short title: context and summary of the problem
   (e.g. "Matrix-1000: STORE grayed out on bank 0")
2. Matrix-Control version (logo → ABOUT…)
3. System (name + version) and computer type
4. Standalone or plugin (AU / VST3) + DAW and its version if needed
5. Synth (Matrix-1000/6/6R) + EPROM / firmware if known
6. Steps to reproduce, numbered
7. What you expected, and what happened
8. Always, or only sometimes?
9. Exact text of the footer message or dialog
10. If possible: a screenshot

Do not send passwords, licences, or a full disk copy.
